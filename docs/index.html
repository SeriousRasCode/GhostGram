01 / The philosophy

Messaging apps were built to keep us connected. GhostGram is built to help us stay focused. All the important updates, none of the pressure to always be online.

02 / Designed with intention

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Less like an inbox. More like your favorite Sunday paper — thoughtfully organized for the way you want to read.

04 / Privacy, by design

Stay informed.
Stay in your space.

GhostGram is built around a simple idea: keeping up with your communities shouldn't require you to be part of every conversation. Browse in a calmer, read-only space.

✓
Read-only interactionNo composing or sending messages from the reading interface.
✓
Powered by TDLibConnects to Telegram through its client library, directly from your device.
✓
Your selection, your feedChoose which channels and groups appear. Leave the rest out.
✓
Open to inspectionExplore the source code, build it yourself, and follow development on GitHub.
